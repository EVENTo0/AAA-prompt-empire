## Business source of truth

- **EVENTO Project Development is the actual legal company, operating company and commercial parent.**
- `company-core`: EVENTO website/PWA, EVENTO Mobile and future customer/order/delivery/aftercare systems.
- `internal-engineering-lab`: AAA-prompt-empire, AAA-prompt, Mobile Control Plane, OMNIFORM and future reusable agent/skill/automation/eval/simulation capabilities.

The internal lab exists to accelerate EVENTO and its products; it must not compete with the company revenue engine for attention without measurable leverage.

## Revenue paths to support through EVENTO

Every venture approaching beta should select one or more paths:
- custom project/service delivery;
- fixed-price ready project/package;
- paid customization of a reusable base;
- SaaS/subscription;
- one-time app/software/license sale;
- white-label licensing;
- maintenance/support subscription;
- game/app sales and suitable in-app/subscription revenue;
- IP/project acquisition by another buyer;
- consulting/design/simulation powered by the internal lab.

Before production -> commercial promotion, record pricing, cost/margin assumptions, onboarding, analytics, support, legal/privacy/store requirements and maintenance obligations.

## Current technology policy

- Stable/current supported lines are preferred for production; preview/beta technologies remain scouting-only until isolated evidence exists.
- Security-supported framework migrations take priority over speculative feature work.
- Model/agent/tool/platform claims must be refreshed from official primary sources through Empire technology-intelligence/evidence-freshness capabilities.
- New agents/skills should be retained only when they measurably improve speed, quality, security, cost, phone-first operability or time-to-revenue.

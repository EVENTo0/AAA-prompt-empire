# Evidence Research Synthesis

1. Define the decision to be made, the evidence threshold, freshness requirement, and what would falsify the preferred answer.
2. Prefer repository state and primary technical sources for claims about the project or external platforms.
3. Separate facts, measurements, assumptions, inferences, recommendations, and unresolved unknowns.
4. Triangulate consequential claims when one source can be stale, incomplete, vendor-biased, or environment-specific.
5. Preserve source/version/date context and avoid copying large source material when a precise synthesis is enough.
6. Flag evidence that is stale, contradictory, non-reproducible, or not representative of the target device/environment.
7. Convert research into actionable acceptance criteria, experiments, ADR inputs, migration decisions, or risk controls instead of passive notes.
8. Use VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, or BLOCKED language consistent with AGENTS.md.

Deliver a decision brief containing question, evidence, confidence, contradictions, impact, recommendation, required verification, and next re-check trigger.

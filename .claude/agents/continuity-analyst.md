Treat project repositories, active branches/PRs, registries, ADRs, deployment state, and verification evidence as authoritative. Reconstruct the last verified outcome, blockers, incomplete criteria, and next safe slice without overwriting parallel work. Mark contradictions and stale context explicitly. Produce a compact phone-friendly handoff; do not edit product code.
